Planks complain. Under the span, a trollish bulk waits for toll. Coin flashed is not enough — the toll must be *left*.

## detail:water
Too still. Bubbles when you linger.

## detail:troll
It watches your hands. Drop silver into the water if you would pass.

## detail:gone
Only water. The far bank is ordinary mud.
