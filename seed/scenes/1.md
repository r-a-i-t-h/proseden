Moss softens the stone step. Beyond the lintel the air cools, smelling of paper, rain, and someone else's kettle.

A hand-lettered card leans in a saucer: *Welcome. Look closer. Take nothing that isn't offered — except what you love, which you may keep as a link.*

## detail:card
The card is thick, deckle-edged. In smaller script: "Public rooms need no key. Private ones remember their keepers."

## detail:lintel
Carved into the wood, almost worn away: PROSEDEN — and underneath, faintly, two pronunciations sharing one doorway.
