A bay of shelves curves around a single deep chair. Light arrives sideways through a tall window, striping the floorboards. Somewhere a clock refuses to hurry.

## detail:window
Rain beads and slides. Outside: a suggestion of garden, more green than geometry.

## detail:chair
The chair knows the shape of long afternoons. A threadbare armrest has been mended twice, carefully.
